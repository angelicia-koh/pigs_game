How it works 

1. Prompt urser to input players (2-4 players game)
2. Get player 1 to start, till the last player turn ends
3. PLayer will be prompt to roll the die
4. If rolled a 1, player turn ends and their score will be 0
5. Else, they get to roll as many times as they wish to. As long as player does not roll a 1
6. And if they exceed 20 points, they also lose
7. So players have to choose when they would like to exit the roll to keep theor score
8. To win, players need to either hit 20 or the highest score cloest to 20
