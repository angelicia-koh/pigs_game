How it works 

1. Prompt user to input players (2-4 players game)
2. Get player 1 to start, till the last player's turn ends
3. Player will be prompted to roll the die
4. If rolled a 1, player's turn ends and their score will be 0
5. Else, they get to roll as many times as they wish to. As long as the player does not roll a 1
6. And if they exceed 20 points, they also lose
7. So players have to choose when they would like to exit the roll to keep their score
8. To win, players need to either hit 20 or the highest score closest to 20
